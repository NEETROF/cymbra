# add-site-italian-locale — cymbra.app in Italian

## Why

Change 41 of the [Italian programme](../../../docs/lingua/italian-programme.md), stage 3: Italian
speakers. The public site (`apps/site`) is in French at the root, English under `/en/` and Spanish
under `/es/`. When the Italian interface ships with en-it (change 45), it links an Italian reader to:
- the privacy policy, from Réglages and the online translation's consent;
- the account deletion page;
- the leaving page, which the browser opens after the extension is removed.

Cymbra ID's Italian e-mails link the terms and privacy pages (I10, change 43). Today all of these point
an Italian reader at the English pages. Italian visitors also have no home or Music page in their
language, though Cymbra Music's app is already in Italian (`app_it.arb`, 929 keys).

The leaving page cannot wait. The extension compiles its address and registers it with the browser,
which opens it after the extension is gone, so no later update can correct it. It must exist, and be
pinned, before the first build that names it.

## What Changes

- **An Italian locale** (design D1): `/it/` beside `/en/` and `/es/`, its own slugs, each page naming its
  translations, the switch and `hreflang` with them, a localised 404.
- **The pages Lingua and Cymbra ID send Italian readers to** (D2, D3):
  - `/it/privacy/`, `/it/termini/`, `/it/assistenza/` and `/it/elimina-account/`, translated from the
    French pages;
  - the legal pages open with « Traduzione di cortesia: fa fede la versione francese », linking the
    French page (I13);
  - `/it/lingua/arrivederci/`, the leaving page, with its thanks;
  - all pinned ahead of the extension build and the e-mails that will link them.
- **The home page and Cymbra Music's page** (D4): `/it/` and `/it/music/`, Music's terms from its Italian
  app; the Italian Music gallery and video recorded by the existing harness.
- **The islands in Italian** (D5): sign-in, account deletion, the leaving question and the shared errors;
  `it-IT` dates; `it_IT` for Sign in with Apple. Account, code redemption and checkout stay in French
  and English, as for Spanish.
- **The Lingua page's Italian text** (D6), without its screenshots: `/it/lingua/` is built only once a
  pair glossed in Italian ships, and change 45 brings its media then.
- **The media budget raised to 50 MB** (D7, the owner's answer of 2026-10-22): the Italian Music media
  would not fit in 30 MB, 40 would leave under 1 MB once change 45's Lingua media land, and a visitor loads
  only their own page's media.
- **The backend's leaving thanks** (D8): `thanks_path("it")` sends an Italian answer back to the Italian
  page.
- **Reviewed by the Italian reviewer agent** (D9, I2, I13), not by the owner.
- **Nothing else moves** (D10): every French, English and Spanish page's text is byte for byte; their
  heads gain the Italian alternate.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `site-locales`:
  - ADDED *The pages Lingua sends Italian readers to exist in Italian*, *The home page and Cymbra
    Music's page exist in Italian* and *The Italian home page describes Lingua for the readers it
    serves*;
  - MODIFIED *Each page names its translations* (held by `add-site-spanish-locale`): Italian under
    `/it/`.
- `site-lingua-page`: MODIFIED *The Lingua leaving page asks one question with fixed answers* (held by
  `add-lingua-usage-counts`), with its Italian path; MODIFIED *The Lingua page's media stay within a
  weight budget* (held by `add-site-lingua-showcase`), at 50 MB.
- `site-locales` also: MODIFIED *The home page and Cymbra Music's page exist in Spanish* (held by
  `extend-site-spanish-locale`), whose scenario lists `/music/`'s translations, now with `/it/music/`.
- `site-music-page`: MODIFIED *The Music page shows real screenshots in its language* (held by
  `add-site-music-showcase`), Italian among its languages.

## Impact

- **Products.** The site, and one backend route:

  - `packages/web-auth` (`apple.ts`, `it_IT`);
  - `backend/server/src/web_lingua.rs` (`thanks_path`);
  - `apps/music/store/video/it/`: the Italian showcase video, generated by the existing harness.

  move later.

  changes 21 and 26 (the site's Italian names and `STUDIED.it`).
- **Order**: before changes 42, 43 and 45. Change 45 lists en-it together with `/it/lingua/`'s media.
- **Effort, against 5–9 ideal days**: 6–10 (design, *Effort*).
