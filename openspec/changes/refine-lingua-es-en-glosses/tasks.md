A shortened form is a sense whose gloss opens on « apocopic form of », « apheretic form of », « syncopic form of », « prepositional form of », « pronunciation spelling of » or « eye dialect spelling of », in any case, tagged or not — never a sense keyed on the `apocopic` or `prepositional` tag alone, nor one tagged `alt-of` beside a shortened form's tag under another pointer's wording (« obsolete form of treinta y un »). A case form is a pointer sense of a `pron` entry whose gloss names a case « of » a word and carries a meaning after a colon or a semicolon. The target is the first `alt_of`/`form_of` word, whole (« voy a », « malo bad »), else the gloss's text after the wording up to a comma, a semicolon, a colon, an opening parenthesis or the end; the meaning is the target's `extra`, else the gloss's text after the target past a comma, a semicolon or a colon, or within “ ”; a case form's, the text after its first colon or semicolon. The sense is replaced in its place by that meaning, else by its target's meaning senses in the same part of speech from a target of three letters or more (`_MIN_BASE`), following the target's own pointer once; pointer tags and fields dropped, other tags kept; `_FORM_OF` gains the six wordings.
