## Full description  (App Store ≤4000 · Play ≤4000)

Cymbra Music turns your device into an interactive piano tutor. Load real sheet music and play along — on a connected MIDI keyboard or by following on screen — while Cymbra listens and guides you note by note.

WATCH THE MUSIC YOUR WAY
• Standard staff notation for reading practice
• Synthesia-style falling notes (waterfall) for quick learning
• Switch views anytime

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Wait Mode pauses until you play the right note — no pressure, no falling behind
• Practice hands separately or together, with left/right hand colour coding
• Built-in metronome to lock in your timing

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Real-time scoring rates your accuracy and timing
• Gamified feedback and a session summary after every run

A GROWING LIBRARY
• Bundled public-domain scores, sorted from beginner to advanced
• Choose from high-quality piano sounds, or import your own SoundFont

Connect any USB MIDI keyboard, or play along on screen. Available in English, French, Italian and Spanish.

Start playing today — the best way to learn piano is to play it.
