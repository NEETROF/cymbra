A question about Cymbra Music, or a problem with your account or subscription?
Write to us: every message gets an answer.

## Contact us

**[contact@cymbra.app](mailto:contact@cymbra.app)**

To help us help you quickly, please include if you can:

- your device and its system (for example *iPad, iPadOS 26* or *Mac, macOS 26*);
- the app version;
- what you were doing, and what happened.

## Frequent questions

### My subscription

A subscription is managed from the store that charged it:

- **App Store**: Settings → your name → *Subscriptions*;
- **Google Play**: Play Store → your profile → *Payments & subscriptions*;
- **web purchase**: [Account](/en/account).

After switching devices, open *Subscription* in the app and tap *Restore purchases*.

### My account

- **Forgot your password**: on the sign-in screen, tap *Forgot password?*
- **Delete my account**: in the app (account menu → *Delete account*), or
  [from the website](/en/delete-account).

### My data

What we collect and why: [privacy policy](/en/privacy).
For any request about your data: [privacy@cymbra.app](mailto:privacy@cymbra.app).
